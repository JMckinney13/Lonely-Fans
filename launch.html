Launch Prep Checklist

Items to complete before accepting real money or public traffic.

  • Form LLC and open business bank account
  • Register domain and adult-friendly hosting (avoid Vercel/Netlify for production adult content)
  • Integrate real age verification (Yoti, Veriff, or VerifyMy)
  • Connect adult payment processor (CCBill or Segpay) for SparkCoin purchases
  • Set up creator payouts (bank or crypto) + tax forms (W-9 / 1099)
  • Complete 2257 record-keeping process and appoint a custodian of records
  • Register DMCA agent with the U.S. Copyright Office
  • Final attorney review of Terms, Privacy, and 2257 policy
  • SSL certificate, basic analytics, and error monitoring
  • Moderation plan and content reporting workflow
