18 U.S.C. § 2257 Attestation

As a creator you are the primary producer of your content. You must confirm the following before going live or receiving payouts:

  • Every person appearing in my content was 18 years of age or older at the time of production.
  • I maintain government-issued ID records for all performers for a minimum of 7 years.
  • I will not upload content involving minors or anyone who appears to be under 18.
